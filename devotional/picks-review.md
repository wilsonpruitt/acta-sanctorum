# Picks Review — Phase 0 Curation (COMPLETE, AASS-DATE AUDITED)

Editorial pass over `saints-picks.csv` for Jan 1 – Nov 10 (315 days).

**Curation bias:**
1. Lean toward obscure AASS-period saints; famous-name-of-the-date is not the default.
2. Exclude saints judged historically dubious in the 1969 *Calendarium Romanum*
   audit (Prisca, Marius/Martha, Julian & Basilissa, Valentine, Blaise's *Acts*,
   Dorothy of Caesarea, Pancras, Susanna of Rome, etc.).
3. **Every pick scheduled on its AASS-native feast date, not the revised Roman
   calendar.** No modern post-Bollandist saints — they live on the website in
   the full AASS but not in the devotional.

**Preface note (for the printed book):** Some famous saints whose modern
calendar feasts fall in Jan–Nov 10 are absent here because their AASS day
is in Dec (Thomas the Apostle), or they post-date the Bollandists
(Thérèse, Kolbe, JPII, Pius X, Korean Martyrs, North American Martyrs).
All remain present in the full AASS at actasanctorum.org.

**Status:** All 315 days locked. CSV updated with `Source` column pointing to
`src/latin/<slug>/day-NN` for Phase 1 drafting. Nov 11 – Dec 31 preserved
but marked out of scope.
